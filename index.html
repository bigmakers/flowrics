Flowrics

Danmaku Lyrics Music Player

Flowrics is a music player that displays scrolling danmaku-style lyrics on screen while playing Apple Music tracks.

Try Flowrics WebRuns in your browser with YouTube videos and synced lyrics from LRCLIB. No install needed.

Features

  • Search and play Apple Music tracks
  • Synced lyrics - words flow across the screen in real time
  • Music video background playback
  • Repeat mode (single / all)
  • Japanese / English language support

Premium

  • My Playlists
  • Background color customization
  • Font customization
