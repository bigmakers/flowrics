Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 31, 2026

Overview

Flowrics ("the App") is developed by Daisaku Harasaki. This privacy policy explains how the App handles your information.

Data Collection

Flowrics does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The App does not use analytics, tracking, or advertising frameworks.

Apple Music

The App accesses your Apple Music library and playback features through Apple's MediaPlayer framework. This access is used solely to play music and display lyrics on your device. No music data or listening history is transmitted to external servers.

Lyrics

The App fetches song lyrics from the third-party service lrclib.net using song title and artist name. No personal information is sent in these requests.

iTunes Search API

The App uses Apple's iTunes Search API to search for songs and music videos. Search queries contain only song titles and artist names entered by the user.

In-App Purchases

The App offers a premium upgrade through Apple's StoreKit framework. All purchase transactions are handled by Apple. The developer does not have access to your payment information.

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or social media SDKs.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at: flowrics@example.com
