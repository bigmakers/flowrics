Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Overview

Flowrics ("the App") is developed by Daisaku Harasaki. This privacy policy explains how the App handles your information.

Data Collection

Flowrics does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to the developer. There is no developer server, and the App does not use analytics, tracking, or advertising frameworks.

Apple Music

The App uses iOS "Media & Apple Music" access through Apple's MediaPlayer framework to play songs and list your library playlists. No listening history or music data is transmitted to the developer.

Song Search and Top Songs

The App uses Apple's iTunes Search API to search for songs, albums, and music videos, and Apple Music's public top-songs chart to show popular songs. Requests contain only your search terms or the song title and artist name.

Lyrics

The App fetches lyrics for the playing song from the third-party service lrclib.net using the song title and artist name. No personal information is sent in these requests.

Lyric Video Typefaces

Typefaces used to draw the lyric video are loaded from Google Fonts.

Data Stored on Your Device

Recently played songs and your settings are stored only on your device.

In-App Purchases

Since version 2.0, all features are free and the App offers no in-app purchases.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact
