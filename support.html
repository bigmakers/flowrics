Flowrics Support

Requirements

Flowrics requires iOS 26 or later and an active Apple Music subscription to play music.

FAQ

Q: Why can't I play music?
A: Please make sure you have an active Apple Music subscription and have allowed Flowrics to access "Media & Apple Music" in the iOS Settings app.
Q: The lyric video does not appear.
A: Lyrics come from LRCLIB. Songs without synced lyrics there cannot be shown as a lyric video. If only plain lyrics are available, they are shown as text instead.
Q: How do I change the look of the lyric video?
A: Tap the style name at the top-left to get a new "Surprise" style, or open Settings (top-right) for Surprise, Reroll, chorus emphasis, and background options.
Q: I bought Premium in an earlier version.
A: Since version 2.0, every feature is free for everyone. If you have questions about a past purchase, please contact us.
Q: How do I change the language?
A: Open Settings (top-right) and choose the language.
