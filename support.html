Flowrics Support

Requirements

Flowrics requires an active Apple Music subscription to play music. iOS 16.0 or later is required.

FAQ

Q: Why can't I play music?
A: Please ensure you have an active Apple Music subscription and have granted the app permission to access Apple Music.
Q: Lyrics are not showing up.
A: Not all songs have synced lyrics available. The app searches for lyrics automatically, but some songs may only have plain text lyrics or none at all.
Q: How do I restore my premium purchase?
A: Open the settings panel and tap the "Restore" button next to the premium purchase section.
Q: How do I change the language?
A: Tap the "EN" or "JP" button in the top-right corner of the settings panel to switch between English and Japanese.
